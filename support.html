Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify my KU email?

Sign up with your @student.ku.edu.np email address. You'll receive a verification link in your inbox. Click the link to confirm your email and complete your account setup.

Why do I need to verify my email?

Email verification ensures that only Kathmandu University students can join KuNect. This keeps our community safe and authentic.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account anytime through Settings > Delete Account in the app. Your personal information will be removed within 30 days. Posts may be retained in anonymized form for community continuity.

How do I request my data?

You can request a copy of your personal data by emailing billjeshbaidya@gmail.com. We'll provide your data within 30 days.

How do I block or report someone?

You can report users or content directly in the app. Go to the user's profile or the post, tap the menu (three dots), and select "Report". We'll review all reports and take appropriate action.

Are my messages private?

Yes, your private messages are between you and the recipient. We do not read or share your messages. For anonymous posts, your real identity is kept secret from other users.

Is my data safe?

Yes! We use industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for all data transmission. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt. Check our Privacy Policy for complete details on how we protect your information.

How do I change my password?

Go to Settings > Account > Change Password in the app. Enter your current password and your new password. Your password will be updated immediately.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password?" Enter your email address and we'll send you a password reset link. Check your email and follow the instructions.

Can I use multiple accounts?

Each person should have one account linked to their KU email. Using multiple accounts violates our Terms of Service and may result in account suspension.

How do I turn off notifications?

You can manage notifications in two ways:

  • In the app: Settings > Notifications > Toggle notification types on/off
  • On your device: Settings > Apps > KuNect > Notifications > Disable

How do I edit my profile?

Tap your profile picture or "Profile" in the app. Tap "Edit Profile" to change your bio, interests, profile picture, or other information. Save your changes when done.

What is the poke feature?

The poke feature is a fun, low-pressure way to show someone you're interested in them. Tap the 👋 button on their profile to send a poke. They'll be notified and can poke you back!

Can I post anonymously?

Yes! You can post in the "Gossip" section with a randomly generated anonymous username. Your real identity is kept secret from other users, but we keep records for moderation purposes.

How does the leaderboard work?

The weekly leaderboard shows which students received the most pokes in the past 7 days. It resets every Monday. It's a fun way to see who's making connections on campus!
